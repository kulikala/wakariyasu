# Failures each technique tends to produce

## Collapsible sections

Collapsible sections suit details that only some readers look at. When content every reader needs is collapsed, readers finish the page without opening it.

## Badges

- Even with badges, keep the start of the body text aligned across items, so that readers can compare items down the page.
- When badges differ only by color, the difference disappears for readers who have trouble telling colors apart and in black-and-white printouts. Make the difference readable in words too.
- When information of different kinds, such as people, states and items, shares a color, readers take information of one kind for the same thing as information of another.

## View switches

Open a view switch on the view readers need first.

## Timelines

- Compute the positions of bars and of the scale from the dates with the same calculation. Positions set separately drift apart.
- Place a note attached to a bar where it does not overlap the bar's name.
- Draw bars and their names in front of background lines (the scale, deadlines, the current point in time), so that the lines do not cut through the names.

## Flow diagrams

Limit one diagram to the elements that support the page's conclusion, and show the rest separately as detail.
